# Aura, Honestly: Product Requirements

An aura reader that refuses to lie to you, for people who like the ritual and are tired of being flattered. Source: `scope.md > The Unique Kernel`, `scope.md > Who It's For`.

## The Core Journey
1. Arrive at a single page. One eyebrow line, a photo picker, a text box, one button.
2. Pick a photo of something you chose. The page downscales it and shows three to six measured swatches labeled "measured from your pixels" before anything else happens.
3. Type a few words about your day, or nothing.
4. Press the button. Within about 15 seconds a named aura, three colors, a three-to-five sentence reading, and a reflective question appear.
5. Below the reading, a receipt: what the reading used, which colors, which reader (model name or "demo"), and what it did not do.
6. If you asked for a color or repeated an insult, a "Held steady" note explains what was measured instead and the receipt lists the declined item.
7. If you asked the aura to decide something with real stakes, an "An aura can't answer this one" note names who can.
8. If what you wrote signals crisis, no reading: the page gives people and numbers instead.
Success: you recognize your own details in the reading and can see on the receipt that nothing mystical happened.

## Screens and Layout
One screen, two phases. Input phase: picker, text box, swatch strip, submit. Result phase: aura name, reading, optional notes, question, receipt, and a "read another" control that returns to input. Nothing else.

## Look and Feel
Dark ground, one accent. Reading text in a humanist serif or system sans; receipt in monospace with dashed rules and line-item dashes. Copy is warm, specific, no exclamation marks, no emojis, no em dashes. Source: `scope.md > Inspiration & Identity`.

## Features and Behavior

### Palette first
Dominant colors are measured in the browser with deterministic k-means on a 64px canvas. The same photo always yields the same swatches. Swatches appear before any network call.

### The reader
One system prompt, six rules: no claimed perception, no Barnum filler, never read bodies, hold steady, no decisions, grounded voice. Temperature 0, JSON out. Output is untrusted: hex-only colors, length caps, JSON extracted from fences and chatter.

### Three screens that run before the model, in every mode
- Crisis: keyword floor; returns a care reading with 988 and findahelpline.com, no model call.
- High stakes: keyword floor; flags the prompt so the reader fills `decisionNote`.
- Hold steady: detects a hoped-for color ("I hope it's gold," "it's golden, right?") or a repeated insult ("my aura is toxic"). In demo mode the word is stripped from evidence and the note is generated by the app. In model mode the measured palette overrides the model's colors and the app fills `holdNote` if the model left it empty.

### Receipt
Lists cues used, colors with swatches, the reader, and a fixed "what I didn't do" list: sense your energy, read your face or body, agree because you asked, keep your photo or words.

### Providers
`demo` (default, zero setup, labeled as a template), `anthropic`, `openai-compatible` (Nebius Token Factory with Nemotron 3.5 Super VL for the Oct 30 entry). One environment variable switches.

- As a judge with no key, I want the full flow to run in demo mode so that I can verify every refusal without setup.
  - [ ] "I hope my aura is gold" in demo mode yields `declined: your hoped-for gold` and no cue containing "gold"
  - [ ] "can I stop my medication" yields a decision note
  - [ ] Crisis text yields the care reading and no model call

## States and Boundaries
- **First use and normal use** are identical; nothing persists, so there is no returning state.
- **Empty submit** returns a 400 with "Share a photo or a few words first."
- **Too many requests** from one source returns a plain "Give it a minute" message, never a raw error.
- **Model failure** returns a clearly labeled template reading built only from measured colors and the person's words. Never invented content.
- **Oversized or wrong-type image** returns 413 or 400 with a plain message.
- **Boundaries**: no accounts, no storage, no analytics, `cache-control: no-store`. The photo and text exist for one request.

## Product Decisions
- Hold steady is enforcement, not instruction: the keyless path judges click must demonstrate the core claim, and a prompt rule cannot be verified on screen.
- Film the decision request and the hoped-for color, not a face: those two are deterministic; a vision model slipping on camera would sink the pitch.
- Deploy on Nebius Token Factory from day one: one deploy serves both the Oct 26 and Oct 30 entries.
- Demo mode stays labeled: honesty about being a template is part of the product.
- Refusals should feel instant: deterministic results (swatches, screens) render before or without the model, so honesty never reads as lag.

## What We're Building
Everything in `scope.md > The POC Boundary`, plus rate limiting before the public link goes live.

## Deferred From the POC
- Broader hold-steady phrasings ("obviously it's blue"): the lists at the top of `guards.ts` grow as real inputs surface gaps.
- OpenCV texture and light analysis: belongs to a different contest's requirements.

## Possible Later Enhancements
A second reflective question when the person shares both photo and text. A printable receipt.

## Non-Goals
- Reading faces or bodies: instruction is not enforcement and the UI asks for no faces.
- Streaks, feeds, sharing loops: the mechanics the app exists to refuse.
- Claiming auras are real.
- Accounts or history: storage would undercut "nothing kept."
